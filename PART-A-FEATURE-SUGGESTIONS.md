# Part A — Suggested Coy Core Features

These are initial product suggestions to validate while personally exploring the Coy app flows (OTP, adding a crush, email verification, invite link and extra-slots screen).

## 1. Crush-status clarity and guidance
Add a small, privacy-safe status guide explaining the stages of a crush entry (for example, invite sent, verification needed, or ready), with clear next steps and resend/retry affordances where applicable. This reduces confusion during the core setup flow and helps users understand what they can do without revealing another person's activity or likes.

## 2. Thoughtful invite and referral flow
Offer a shareable invite flow with clear preview text, optional contact sharing initiated by the user, and a lightweight invite-progress view that only shows safe delivery/claim statuses. This can make onboarding easier, help users invite people they already know, and improve activation while respecting privacy.

**Important:** These are hypotheses, not findings from hands-on app exploration. Before submitting Part A, sign up and explore the specified Coy flows, then adjust the feature rationale to refer to real friction you personally observe.
